Samajh gaya. Single page rahega, to saare sections ek hi scroll timeline mein aayenge. Car 3D mein peeche rahegi aur text sections uske upar scroll ke saath badlenge. Tumhara teardown ab ek chapter ban jayega, poori site nahi.

Reels ke liye kuch cheezein hata di hain, kyunki single scroll page mein wo kaam nahi karti ya video mein dikhti nahi: news section, cookie banner, privacy page aur real contact form. Unki jagah ek “Book a test drive” CTA aayega.

Phase 1: Foundation (brand, hero, navbar, stats)
Fictional brand ka naam, wordmark logo, color (dark + champagne/silver accent) aur luxury fonts.
Premium navbar: transparent se blur-glass, aur menu click par us chapter tak smooth scroll.
Hero: brand name, tagline, “Explore” button.
Stats strip: 0-100, top speed, power, weight, count-up animation ke saath.
Scroll length badhegi (pages=6 se lagbhag 14) aur stages.js aur site.js ko naye chapter structure ke liye rewrite kiya jayega.
Footer: model credit aur “Built by Rajan”.

Phase 1 ke baad: site brand wali dikhegi aur hero, stats aur teardown sahi chalenge.

Phase 2: Content chapters
Design philosophy: bade close-up camera shots (headlight, wheel, profile) aur short text.
Engineering: tumhara current teardown, naye timing ke hisaab se adjust.
Powertrain: spec table aur power-torque curve chart.
Interior and craftsmanship chapter.
Heritage timeline (1975 se aaj tak).
Lineup: 3 model cards.

Phase 2 ke baad: poori story ek scroll mein, hero se heritage tak, bina atke chalegi.

Phase 3: Configurator, polish, reels aur deploy
Configurator: paint color aur wheel swatches, car live badlegi.
Gallery chapter aur “Book a test drive” CTA (mailto ya free Web3Forms).
Custom cursor, hover effects aur sound toggle.
Reel mode: ek button jo UI chhupa de aur auto-scroll chala de, taaki OBS se clean recording ho.
Mobile 9:16 layout check, SEO meta aur Open Graph image.
Performance tuning, GitHub push aur Vercel/Netlify free deploy.

Phase 3 ke baad: live link ready hoga aur reels record ho sakengi.
